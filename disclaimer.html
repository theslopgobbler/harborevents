Check before you go

Harbor Events gathers listings from chamber calendars, venues, city and county websites, local news and community tips. We check them, but details change: events get cancelled, moved, rescheduled, or sell out, sometimes after we post them.

Always confirm the date, time, place, price and ticket availability with the organizer before you go. Use each event's link, which goes to the organizer's page whenever one exists. A listing here does not guarantee that an event will happen, that tickets or space are available, or that the information is complete.

Sold-out, ticketed and 21+ events

Events marked SOLD OUT are shown for information only. For other ticketed events, tickets may run out at any time, and we can't guarantee they're still available. Ticket sales, refunds and prices are handled by the organizer or ticket seller, not by Harbor Events.

A red 21+ tag marks events we know are for adults 21 and over, such as wine events and late-night bar shows. It's a courtesy, not a guarantee: an untagged event may still have age limits, and the organizer or venue decides who can attend and may ask for ID.

Removing and declining listings

  • We may edit, hide or remove any listing at any time, for any reason, at the site owner's discretion.
  • Submitted events may be declined for any reason. We don't list events that aren't open to the public.
  • Religious services and religious events are not accepted or listed at this time. Exceptions may be made at the site owner's discretion, including but not limited to civic events such as the annual Mayor's Prayer Breakfast.
  • By submitting an event, you confirm the details are accurate and give us permission to publish them here and on our Discord.
  • Organizers who want a listing corrected or removed can ask on our Discord.
